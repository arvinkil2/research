Working Paper 19

Bonds and Stocks: Twenty Years of Relative Market Size Across Ten Economies

Kilambi Research

October 2026

Download PDF

Abstract

Bond markets exceed equity markets in 9 of 10 economies; the US is the sole exception with its ratio falling from 1.22 to 0.89. China's bond market grew 24-fold to $28.7 trillion; equities run 2 to 7 times hotter than bonds everywhere, so the ratio moves on the equity leg.
